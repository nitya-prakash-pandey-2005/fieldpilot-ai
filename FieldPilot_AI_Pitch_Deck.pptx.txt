01 · THE PROBLEM
Site inspection is manual, slow, and paper-heavy
01
Workers stop to document
Pull out a tablet, take photos, fill forms by hand — tools down, progress paused.
02
RFIs take days
A single deviation triggers paperwork, spec lookups, and email chains before anyone acts.
03
Hazards get missed
Missing PPE, unguarded edges and spacing errors are spotted late — if at all.
04
Rework is expensive
A 40mm rebar deviation caught after a pour costs six figures to fix.
Construction inspection today is slow, manual and paper-heavy. Walk through the four pain points. The image is a real site — rebar, formwork, workers with hands full.
2

02 · THE SOLUTION
The AI watches, detects, and files the paperwork — hands-free
PASSIVE
Hands stay on the tools
Meta smart glasses stream what the worker sees. No tablets, no manual photos, no interrupting the build.
AUTOMATIC
10 agents do the work
Vision, measurement, compliance and safety agents catch deviations and auto-draft the RFI with the spec quoted.
INSTANT
Spoken back in real time
The worker hears the verdict through the glasses' open-ear speaker: "Stop work — rebar spacing 40mm above spec."
WHY IT'S NEW
Procore and Autodesk make workers do the inspection. FieldPilot does it for them — passively — and predicts the next failure before it happens.
Our answer: passive, hands-free inspection. The glasses watch, the AI detects and files the paperwork, the worker keeps working and just hears what to do.

03 · MULTI-AGENT DATA FLOW
From captured frame to spoken alert — Agent 1 to 10
Walk the pipeline left to right: glasses capture, pocket phone relays (works offline on the NPU), cloud runs the ten-agent system, notification agent sends a TTS alert back to the worker's ear. Closed loop from sight to speech.
